# Roadmap

The order is intentional: prove the experience before adding scale.

## 0. Repository foundation

Agreed direction, approved art reference, decision log, contributor flow, issue/PR templates, and automatic foundation checks.

## 1. Shared combat/UI lab

A deliberately designed small area, coherent pixel assets, player plus two companions, clear selection/targeting, reset, and visible time controls. Test world-moves-when-you-act and real-time-with-pause. The user chooses or requests a focused third experiment after playing.

## 2. One complete expedition

Connect a settlement, trail, camp, and exploration site. Add gathering, a useful discovery, an upgrade, and save/load. Establish combat and defeat rules before increasing content.

## 3. A companion who remembers and acts

Ground conversation in known facts and events. Demonstrate an actual shared experience being recalled later and changing an action. Verify model access, latency, and cost first.

## 4. Observable society

A second settlement, a trade route, a resource dependency, and competing interests. Let the player notice and influence consequences. Measure how offscreen activity advances without losing important people or history.

## 5. Expand

More landscapes, cultures, creatures, technologies, mysteries, and social depth. Expand only while the expedition loop remains enjoyable and the simulation can be inspected and tested.

Planned implementation boundaries: simulation, game/rendering/UI, AI adapters, content/assets. Do not create a large empty framework before the demos establish their needs.
